About me

A practical developer with a people-first approach.

Motivated and adaptable Web Developer with hands-on experience building modern, responsive and performance-focused WordPress websites for clients across different industries. At Core Concepts, I combine Elementor and Elementor Pro with custom HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP adjustments to turn designs and business requirements into flexible, maintainable websites. My work includes dynamic content with ACF, Custom Post Types, reusable templates, contact forms, SMTP and third-party integrations, alongside responsive testing, on-page SEO, performance improvements and ongoing maintenance. With a Computer Science background and previous experience in client-facing, hospitality and talent-sourcing roles, I bring clear communication, reliability, organisation and a practical problem-solving mindset to every project.
