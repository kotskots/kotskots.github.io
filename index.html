About me

Design and development, end to end.

I'm a WordPress developer based in Thessaloniki who cares about websites that look and feel impressive. At Core Concepts and ArtAbout I personally handled both the design and the development of the client websites assigned to me, from HTML mockups and design systems to responsive Elementor builds, scroll-triggered animations and interactive sections.

When a site needs something no theme or plugin offers, I build it: custom WordPress plugins, widgets and JavaScript components, developed with AI-assisted tools like Claude Code. I define what the feature must do, structure it, integrate it into the site and test it on every device. I'm looking for a WordPress or web developer role, remote or in Thessaloniki, and I'm also available for freelance projects.
